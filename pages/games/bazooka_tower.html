Bazooka Tower



Climb the Everkneeler Tower and bring wrath to the scum living on its very top.
Bazooka Tower is an arcade-style shooter platformer. You play as Vincent Everkneeler, making his way through the tower one floor at a time. Armed with your Infinity Bazooka, mow down dirtbags that stand in your way.
The tower is made up of rooms, and their layout is different on each playthrough. You must defeat all the enemies in a room in order to advance to the next room. Be on the lookout for goodies dropped by your foes, like money you can spend at the shop, items that improve your firepower or provide a temporary effect, health items, or powerful weapons with limited ammo.
The road to the top won't be without peril - you'll meet endless hazards and an army of thugs wanting nothing more than to see your demise. They can't be reasoned with: the only language they understand is violence, so give them a piece of your mind!

Controls
- Move: Arrow Keys
- Jump: Z
- Shoot from bazooka: X
- Shoot from secondary weapon: C

Note: Bazooka Tower is in early alpha.
Many features and assets have not been developed yet, and the game may be unstable or contain bugs.
